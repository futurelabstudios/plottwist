# Anime illustration direction

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. The generated originals are retained; the project assets are WebP copies without geometric changes.

## The Last Signal — generation prompt

Use case: illustration. Asset type: a production illustration atlas for PlotTwist's science-fiction comic THE LAST SIGNAL, no text. Create ONE landscape 2:1 image composed of exactly 8 equally sized square illustrations arranged 4 columns by 2 rows, perfectly aligned, no gutters or exterior margins. Each square composition must be independently complete, not a scene extending across cells. High-taste anime concept sketch: delicate graphite and dark plum ink linework, subtle crosshatching, soft translucent lavender watercolor, cinematic expressive faces, quiet elegance, no stick figures, no photorealism, no heavy neon. Consistent cast: South Asian young adult captain with short wavy black hair and dark violet jacket, engineer Imani a Black woman with short coils and teal work jacket, Ren a tired East Asian male pilot with loose dark hair. Midnight-violet paper backgrounds, pale lavender light, turquoise instrument glow and tiny lime accents, matching a premium dark comic platform. Top row left to right: 1 wide courier spacecraft approaching a remote violet storm station under a ringed planet; 2 captain and Imani on bridge examining an incoming signal; 3 close portrait of worried Ren on a communications screen, cracked console; 4 abandoned identical spacecraft floating inside station hangar. Bottom row left to right: 5 Imani discovering a memory archive, restrained holographic records; 6 captain Ren and Imani discussing a luminous transmitter in a chamber; 7 two spacecraft joined by a rescue tether escaping through storm shield corridor; 8 peaceful dawn returning courier ship with captain looking at distant planet. Characters and ships consistent across all cells. Artwork only, NO typography, labels, captions, logos, frames, panels borders, speech bubbles or watermark. The 8 cells are for CSS cropping.

## The Last Ember — generation prompt

Use case: illustration. Asset type: a production illustration atlas for PlotTwist's fantasy comic THE LAST EMBER, no text. Create ONE landscape 2:1 image composed of exactly 8 equally sized square illustrations arranged 4 columns by 2 rows, perfectly aligned, no gutters or exterior margins. Each square is independently complete. High-taste anime sketchbook style: delicate graphite and plum ink linework, subtle crosshatching, soft translucent lavender watercolor with restrained ember gold, pale jade and turquoise accents. Midnight-violet backgrounds, artful negative space, expressive quiet faces, cinematic composition, no photorealism, no stick figures, no heavy neon. Consistent cast: young South Asian traveler with shoulder-length dark hair, charcoal cloak and small satchel; gentle ancient dragon with slender elegant silhouette, pale teal scales and gold eyes. Top row left to right: 1 sleeping old city and bell tower beneath two moons; 2 traveler listening to dragon coiled around tower; 3 luminous ember heart suspended inside tower with delicate chains; 4 traveler deciphering subtle moon runes as bells turn. Bottom row left to right: 5 dragon lowering head to traveler in trust; 6 softly luminous silhouettes of forgotten citizens gathered around ember; 7 traveler and dragon opening tower gates with a protective veil; 8 dawn over waking city, dragon flying free and traveler foreground. Same traveler, dragon and architecture across cells. Artwork only, NO typography, labels, captions, logos, frames, panel borders, speech bubbles or watermark. The 8 cells are for CSS cropping.

## Proportions and tile selection

Both Play atlases have a 4 × 2 grid of square cells. `AnimeStoryArt` draws an SVG with a square viewBox and `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid meet"`. Width is responsive and height follows the cell's original ratio. Story captions and dialogue remain accessible HTML, outside the art.

The Last Signal uses all eight scenes across five pages. The Last Ember uses all eight scenes across three pages. Tile maps live beside the reader markup so scene selections can be revised without regenerating the cast. Work uses its existing eight-scene `life-crossroads-atlas-v2.png`, with square viewBoxes, proportional dimensions and no panel zoom.
