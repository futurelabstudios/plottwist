# The life you make — solo sci-fi edition

The requested direction is a solitary adult man, not romance: a literal
science-fiction world with master’s, entrepreneurship and employment choices.
*Childhood’s End* is a thematic reference for disruptive change and the cost of
an apparently comfortable future, not a source of characters, dialogue or plot.
Publisher reference: https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/28158/childhoods-end-by-arthur-c-clarke/

## Original premise

Seven years after a broken luminous ring appeared in the sky, an automated city
provides comfort without providing purpose. A lonely man chooses a career and
discovers what ambition, uncertainty, contribution and sustainable effort ask
of him. No romantic arc and no partner as a reward or cure for isolation.

The first choice selects three distinct later settings and problems. Later
decisions affect skills, courage, purpose and energy. The ending reflects the
career, with a separate recovery ending for exhaustion. This is fiction, not a
prediction of real career outcomes. Original story data is in `careerStory.ts`.
The separate `plottwist.career-comic.v1` save slot preserves the earlier story.

## Artwork

Built-in image generation; no CLI/API key workflow. Style reference only:
`public/art/borrowed-stars-atlas.png`. Active asset:
`public/art/life-crossroads-atlas-v2.png` (1774 × 887). Prior artwork remains
saved non-destructively, but is no longer referenced by the live comic.

Production prompt specification:

- Use case: illustration-story; premium detailed cinematic graphic novel.
- Exactly one human protagonist in every frame. No romance, couples, women,
  companions, touching or hearts.
- Consistent 27-year-old Indian man, medium-brown skin, messy wavy black hair,
  subtle short beard, tired intelligent brown eyes, dark teal overshirt/coat,
  charcoal tee, copper messenger bag. Dignified introspection, not a cute mascot.
- Original near-future automated Indian megacity with a huge distant thin,
  broken luminous celestial ring. No Overlords, book characters or copied plot.
- Rich ink linework, painterly textures, dimensional fabric and skin, expressive
  faces and hands, amber practical lights against deep teal shadows.
- One 4 × 2 atlas, eight equal square cells, no gutters, dividers or frames.
- Row 1: lonely apartment at blue hour; university observatory/master’s;
  independent maker workshop/business; public transit operations/job.
- Row 2: solitary rain-soaked city setback; solitary rooftop at uncertain dawn;
  thoughtful waist-up portrait; serious, quietly resolute waist-up portrait.
- Keep faces central with uncropped heads. No words, numbers, captions, speech
  bubbles, labels, UI, logos or watermark. No flat SVG, chibi or plastic 3D.

Rendering retains SVG viewports for atlas crops and accessible HTML narration.
Motion is a short cinematic camera drift and atmosphere effects, not full
character animation. Animation off and system reduced-motion stop the effects.
